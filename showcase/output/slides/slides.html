Watch two prices monthly, prepare the 20 countries, and test the rule

Three steps that turn the charts into an early-warning routine.
1 · Monitor

Check oils and cereals every month

The FAO index is published monthly; the rule needs only two numbers.

2 · Prepare

Put the 20 exposed countries first

Pre-position support where hunger is serious and food is imported.

3 · Test

Back-test the rule on held-out years

Fix the rule on early data, then score it on later shocks.
