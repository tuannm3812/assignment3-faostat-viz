The data

  • FAO Food Price Index, monthly, Jan 1990 to Mar 2026, five food groups.
  • Global Hunger Index 2025, 91 countries with import data.
  • World Bank food imports as % of merchandise imports, latest year 2015–2024.

Definitions

  • Shock month: all-food index 20%+ above a year earlier.
  • Signal: oils and cereals both 20%+ above a year earlier, outside a shock.
  • Exposed: GHI 20+ and food imports above the 16.3% median.

Limits

  • The rule was read off the same history: a signal to watch, not a forecast.
  • Machine-learning models trained on pre-2013 data missed most of the 2020–21 rise.
  • Import shares come from different years per country.
